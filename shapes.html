Namespace mbsh:

https://mahabharata.tanay.uk/shapes#

This is the namespace document for https://mahabharata.tanay.uk/shapes#, the SHACL shapes of the SKOS concept scheme Characters of the Mahabharata. Each term is described in the specification; this page forwards a browser to the entry named by the fragment of the IRI, so https://mahabharata.tanay.uk/shapes#ConceptShape opens the entry for Concept. The data is served as RDF Turtle at mahabharata_characters.ttl and the shapes at shapes.ttl.
